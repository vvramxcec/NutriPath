% ======================= ABSTRACT =======================
\begin{center}
    \Large\textbf{ABSTRACT}
\end{center}
\vspace{1cm}
\textbf{``FamilyCare 360''} is a smart family health management and monitoring application designed to help families maintain the health records of all members on a single secure platform. The system allows users to store digital medical records, track health conditions and vital signs, receive medicine reminders, manage hospital appointments and upload prescriptions and medical reports. It also provides a dedicated doctor portal where healthcare professionals can monitor patient progress, review medical history, prescribe medicines,  and schedule follow-up visits. An emergency blood donor search helps find registered donors based on blood group, age, and health status. The system provides role-based access for three types of users: User, Doctor, and System Admin. A User can create and manage the family profile, add and update the details and health records of family members, and view dashboards, appointments, prescriptions, and reminders, while the System Admin manages users and system settings. The application is developed as a web-based system using HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap for the frontend, Python Django for the backend, and MySQL as the database. By bringing together families and doctors on a single platform, the application improves communication, ensures timely healthcare management, and reduces the need for maintaining paper-based medical records. Overall, FamilyCare 360 offers a simple, organized, and efficient solution for managing family healthcare digitally.

\chapter{INTRODUCTION}
\pagenumbering{arabic}

\section{Project Area}
\par
Health is one of the most important concerns of every family, yet the health information of family members is often scattered across paper files, mobile applications, and different hospitals. Prescriptions get misplaced, medicine timings are forgotten, appointments are missed, and during an emergency the required medical history or a matching blood donor cannot be found quickly. Managing healthcare for several family members, including children and elderly people, makes this problem even more difficult.

Conventionally, hospital systems focus on individual clinical visits and records, while remote monitoring tools track the measurements of a single person rather than the whole family. Medicine reminder applications handle only adherence, and blood donor search is usually carried out through separate platforms or social media. Users therefore depend on many disconnected tools, which makes it difficult to maintain a complete and up-to-date picture of the family's health.

``\textbf{FamilyCare 360}'' is proposed as a smart family health management and monitoring system. It brings digital health records, vital sign monitoring, appointments, medicine reminders, prescriptions, doctor consultation, and emergency blood donor search together in one web-based platform with role-based access for the User, the Doctor, and the System Admin. Users can store medical records, prescriptions, and reports for every family member, doctors can review patient history and prescribe medicines and diet plans, and the System Admin can manage users and system settings.

Developed using Python Django and MySQL with an HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap frontend, FamilyCare 360 combines web application development, relational database management, and authentication in a practical healthcare system. Health information is stored securely in the database so that records are organized, accessible, and not lost, and the responsive interface allows the application to be used on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

\section{Objectives}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{To develop a centralized platform for family health records:}
The system aims to provide a single web application where all the medical records, prescriptions, and reports of every family member are stored securely and can be accessed whenever required.

\item \textbf{To record and monitor vital signs:}
The system allows users to record vital signs such as blood pressure, blood sugar, heart rate, temperature, weight, and oxygen level so that health conditions can be tracked over time.

\item \textbf{To manage appointments and reminders:}
The system allows users to manage doctor appointments and provides medicine and appointment reminders so that treatment is followed on time.

\item \textbf{To enable doctor consultation and prescription:}
The system provides a doctor portal where doctors can view patient records, add consultation notes, prescribe medicines, recommend personalized diet plans, and schedule follow-up visits.

\item \textbf{To provide an emergency blood donor search:}
The system allows registered donors to be searched based on blood group, age, and health status so that donors can be found quickly in an emergency.

\item \textbf{To implement role-based access control:}
The system provides separate access and functionalities for the System Admin, Doctor, and User so that every role can access only the information relevant to it.

\item \textbf{To provide secure and persistent data storage:}
The system stores healthcare information securely in a MySQL database with authentication so that records are protected and not lost.
\end{enumerate}

\chapter{LITERATURE REVIEW}

\section{Does Remote Patient Monitoring Reduce Acute Care Use? A Systematic Review}

\hspace{1cm}This paper presents a systematic review of 91 studies published between 2015 and 2020, collected from PubMed, Embase, and CINAHL, to examine whether remote patient monitoring reduces the use of acute care. The review reports that remote patient monitoring reduced hospital admissions, the length of hospital stays, and emergency visits. This paper is relevant to FamilyCare 360 because it supports the idea that regular monitoring of vital signs outside the hospital can improve healthcare outcomes, which is consistent with the vital monitoring module of the proposed system. However, the included studies were heterogeneous, the evidence for some diseases was limited, and the monitoring was focused on individual patients rather than families. \cite{three}

\section{Do Mobile Device Apps Designed to Support Medication Adherence Demonstrate Efficacy?}

\hspace{1cm}This paper presents a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomised controlled trials on mobile applications that support medication adherence. The meta-analysis included 9 trials with 1,159 participants and found that the applications improved medication adherence, with an odds ratio of about 2.12. This paper is relevant to FamilyCare 360 because it shows that digital medicine reminders can help patients follow their treatment, which supports the medicine reminder module of the proposed system. However, the studies had small samples, short follow-up periods of at most 16 weeks, and relied on self-reported adherence. \cite{two}

\section{Remote Patient Monitoring: A Systematic Review}

\hspace{1cm}This paper presents a systematic review of remote patient monitoring in which 272 of 947 articles retrieved from PubMed (2000--2018) were included. According to the review, about 76.8\% of the studies reported positive results from remote monitoring. This paper is relevant to FamilyCare 360 because it demonstrates the benefits of digital monitoring of patients and supports the need for a platform that tracks health data continuously. However, remote monitoring had not yet been adequately evaluated for postoperative and surgical care, and the systems reviewed monitor an individual patient and do not combine family records, doctor care, and donor search. \cite{one}

\chapter{PROBLEM DEFINITION AND MOTIVATIONS}

\section{Existing System}

Family health information is usually maintained using paper files, photographs of prescriptions, notes, or memory, while appointments and medicine timings are tracked using separate applications or not tracked at all. Hospital systems focus on individual clinical visits and records, and remote monitoring tools track the measurements of one person rather than the whole family. Emergency blood donor search is carried out separately from health management systems, often through personal contacts or social media. As a result, family health data is scattered across paper files, applications, and hospitals, and it is difficult to obtain a complete view when it is needed. FamilyCare 360 addresses these limitations by providing a single role-based web platform for family records, doctor care, reminders, and donor search.

\section{Limitations}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Fragmented Healthcare Records:} Medical records, prescriptions, and reports are scattered across paper files, applications, and hospitals.

\item \textbf{Paper-Based Records Are Easily Lost:} Physical prescriptions and reports can be misplaced, damaged, or forgotten when needed.

\item \textbf{No Family-Level View:} Existing monitoring tools track one person's measurements rather than all members of a family.

\item \textbf{Missed Medicines and Appointments:} Without reminders, medicines and follow-up visits are easily forgotten.

\item \textbf{Weak Doctor--Patient Communication:} Doctors do not have an easy way to review the history and progress of a patient before consultation.

\item \textbf{Emergency Delays:} Finding a suitable blood donor in an emergency takes time because donor search is separate from health management systems.

\item \textbf{No Role-Based Access:} Many tools do not separate the access of users, doctors, and administrators, which affects the privacy of health data.

\item \textbf{Limited Monitoring of Health Trends:} Vital signs are rarely recorded regularly, so changes in health conditions are noticed late.
\end{itemize}

\section{Problem Statement}
\par To solve the problem of managing family health records, medicines, appointments, and doctor communication through a single digital platform, and to reduce fragmented healthcare records and emergency delays.

\section{Proposed System}
\par FamilyCare 360 is a smart family health management and monitoring system developed as a web application. It provides a centralized platform where a User can create a family profile, add family members, record vital signs, store medical reports and prescriptions, manage doctor appointments, and receive medicine and appointment reminders. Doctors can review patient records, add consultation notes, prescribe medicines, recommend personalized diet plans, and schedule follow-up visits, and an emergency blood donor search helps find registered donors. Access is controlled through three roles: User, Doctor, and System Admin. The application is built using Python Django for the backend, MySQL for the database, and HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap for the responsive user interface.

The key features of the proposed system include:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{User Registration and Login:} Allows users, doctors, and administrators to securely sign in and access features according to their role.

\item \textbf{Family Management:} Allows the User to add, edit, delete, and manage family members and their health information.

\item \textbf{Health Records and Vital Signs:} Stores medical history, health conditions, and vital signs such as blood pressure, blood sugar, heart rate, temperature, weight, and oxygen level.

\item \textbf{Appointment Management:} Allows booking and management of doctor appointments with notifications.

\item \textbf{Medicine Reminders:} Reminds users about medicine timings and upcoming appointments.

\item \textbf{Doctor Portal:} Allows doctors to view patient records, add consultation notes, prescribe medicines, and recommend personalized diet plans.

\item \textbf{Medical Report Upload:} Allows users to upload prescriptions and medical reports for each family member.

\item \textbf{Emergency Blood Donor Search:} Allows registered donors to be searched based on blood group, age, and health status.

\item \textbf{Admin Management:} Allows the System Admin to manage users and system settings.

\item \textbf{Secure Storage:} Stores healthcare information securely in a MySQL database.
\end{enumerate}

\chapter{DESIGN AND IMPLEMENTATION}

The proposed system is a web application named FamilyCare 360, designed to help families manage their health records and communicate with doctors. The system provides a single platform where users can store records, monitor vital signs, manage appointments and reminders, search for blood donors, and consult doctors. The application follows a client--server architecture in which the browser acts as the client and a Django server with a MySQL database acts as the backend.

\section{Overall Design}
FamilyCare 360 is a web application designed to combine family health record management with doctor care and emergency support. It uses HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap for the user interface, Python Django for application logic, and MySQL for data storage. Three roles access the system: User, Doctor, and System Admin. Overall, the system aims to provide a simple, secure, and centralized platform that supports continuous monitoring of family health.

\section{System Design}
The system is structured into three main layers: the presentation layer, the application layer, and the data layer.

The presentation layer is developed using HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap and provides interfaces for health records, vital monitoring, appointments, reminders, and blood donor search. The application layer is developed using Python Django and manages authentication, role-based access, health records, appointments, and notifications. The data layer uses MySQL to securely store users, family details, health records, vital signs, appointments, prescriptions, and other healthcare information.

When a user interacts with the system, the request is sent to the Django server, validated for authentication and role, processed, and the result is returned to the user interface. This layered design ensures organized processing, maintainability, and efficient data management.

\subsection{Client-Side Design}
The client side represents the user interface of FamilyCare 360. It is developed using HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap to provide a clean, responsive, and user-friendly experience.

The client side includes pages such as login, registration, user dashboard, family members, health records and vitals, appointments, reminders, reports, blood donor search, doctor dashboard, prescriptions, and admin management. Bootstrap components are used to make the interface responsive on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices, and forms with validation guide users while entering details.

\subsection{Server-Side Design}
The server side is developed using Python Django. It is responsible for authentication, role-based access control, handling of health records and appointments, generating reminders and notifications, and communicating with the MySQL database.

Django models represent users, family members, health records, vital signs, appointments, prescriptions, reports, and donors. Views check the role of the logged-in user before returning data, so that a User can access only their own family's information, a Doctor can access the records of their patients, and the System Admin can manage the system.

\section{System Architecture}
The FamilyCare 360 system follows a three-tier architecture consisting of the Frontend, the Backend, and the Database.

Three user roles access the system: User, Doctor, and System Admin. The frontend provides interfaces for health records, vital monitoring, appointments, reminders, and blood donor search. The backend, built using Python Django, manages authentication, role-based access, health records, appointments, and notifications. The MySQL database securely stores users, family details, health records, vital signs, appointments, prescriptions, and other healthcare information.

\section{Module Description}

\noindent \textbf{FamilyCare 360} is designed as a web application that operates through six modules which work together to provide a complete family healthcare management platform.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{User and Authentication Module:} Manages user login, authentication, and role-based access for the System Admin, Doctor, and User.

    \item \textbf{Family Management Module:} Allows the User to add, edit, delete, and manage family members and family health information.

    \item \textbf{Health Records and Vital Signs Module:} Stores medical history, health conditions, blood pressure, blood sugar, heart rate, temperature, weight, and oxygen level.

    \item \textbf{Appointment and Reminder Module:} Manages doctor appointments, medicine reminders, and appointment notifications.

    \item \textbf{Doctor and Prescription Module:} Allows doctors to view patient records, add consultation notes, prescribe medicines, and recommend personalized diet plans.

    \item \textbf{Medical Reports and Blood Donor Module:} Supports uploading of medical reports and allows registered users to be searched as potential blood donors based on blood group, age, and health status.
\end{enumerate}

\chapter{RESULTS AND DISCUSSION}

\section{Advantages}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Centralized Health Records:}
All medical records, prescriptions, and reports of the family are stored in one secure place.

\item \textbf{Family-Level Monitoring:}
Vital signs and health conditions of every family member can be tracked on a single platform.

\item \textbf{Timely Reminders:}
Medicine and appointment reminders help users follow treatment and avoid missed visits.

\item \textbf{Doctor Collaboration:}
Doctors can review patient history, prescribe medicines, and recommend personalized diet plans.

\item \textbf{Emergency Support:}
The blood donor search helps find suitable donors quickly in an emergency.

\item \textbf{Role-Based Access:}
Separate access for the System Admin, Doctor, and User protects the privacy of health data.

\item \textbf{Reduced Paper Records:}
Digital storage reduces the need to maintain paper-based medical records.

\item \textbf{Responsive Interface:}
The web interface works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
\end{itemize}

\section{Limitations}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Internet Dependency:}
An active internet connection is required to access the web application.

\item \textbf{Manual Data Entry:}
Vital signs and records must be entered manually, and incorrect entries may affect monitoring.

\item \textbf{No Direct Device Integration:}
The system does not yet collect readings automatically from wearable or medical devices.

\item \textbf{Basic Notification Mechanism:}
Reminders and alerts are limited to the application and do not use advanced notification services such as SMS or push notifications.

\item \textbf{Donor Availability:}
Blood donor search depends on donors who have registered in the system.

\item \textbf{Limited Evaluation:}
The application has not yet been evaluated with a large and diverse group of users.
\end{itemize}

\chapter{CONCLUSION AND FUTURE SCOPE}

\section{Conclusion}

\hspace{1cm} FamilyCare 360 provides a centralized platform for managing family healthcare information. It supports digital health records, vital monitoring, appointments, prescriptions, and medicine reminders, and it allows doctors to monitor patient information, provide prescriptions, consultation notes, and personalized diet plans. Role-based access provides appropriate access for the System Admin, Doctor, and User.

By bringing families and doctors together on a single platform, the system improves communication, supports timely healthcare management, reduces the dependence on paper-based records, and addresses the gap identified in the literature between hospital systems, individual monitoring tools, and emergency donor search.

\section{Future Scope}

\begin{enumerate}

\item \textbf{Wearable Device Integration:}
Collecting vital signs automatically from smartwatches and medical devices to reduce manual entry.

\item \textbf{Mobile Application:}
Developing a dedicated Android and iOS application for easier access and push notifications.

\item \textbf{SMS and Email Alerts:}
Adding SMS and email notifications for medicines, appointments, and emergencies.

\item \textbf{Video Consultation:}
Allowing online video consultation between users and doctors.

\item \textbf{Analytics and Health Insights:}
Adding charts and trend analysis of vital signs to help detect health risks early.

\item \textbf{Wider User Evaluation:}
Evaluating the application with a larger and more diverse group of users and improving security measures such as encryption of sensitive records.

\end{enumerate}
